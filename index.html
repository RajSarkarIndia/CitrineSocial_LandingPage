No Recommendation Algorithm

Content isn't ranked by what keeps you scrolling. Your chosen interests determine what appears.

Minimal Tracking

Instead of building a detailed engagement profile, CitrineSocial simply remembers the interests you explicitly select to personalize your feed.

Always in Control

Add or remove interests whenever you like. Your feed changes because you decided—not because an opaque algorithm did.
